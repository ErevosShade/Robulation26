## 3. Robocon Campaign Records (Page 05 — Verbatim)

### Robocon 2013
- **Honor**: **Best ROOKIE Team**
- **Theme**: *Throwing Arrow into Pot*
- **Problem**: Robots had to collect and accurately throw arrows into pots placed at different heights and distances.
- **Team POV**: Success depended on precise aiming, stable launching mechanisms, and repeatable shooting accuracy rather than just speed.

### Robocon 2015
- **Honor**: **Best Non Quaterfinalist** (and **₹150,000 MathWorks Award**)
- **Theme**: *Robominton*
- **Problem**: Robots had to play a badminton-inspired game by launching shuttlecocks into target areas.
- **Team POV**: Focus was on designing an accurate launcher, controlling shot trajectory, and ensuring consistent performance under match conditions.

### Robocon 2016
- **Honor**: **National Representation**
- **Theme**: *Clean Energy Recharging the World*
- **Problem**: A powered robot had to assist and guide a non-powered robot through various arena tasks inspired by clean energy transfer.
- **Team POV**: The biggest challenge was coordination between two robots, requiring reliable communication, synchronization, and innovative mechanical interaction.

### Robocon 2018
- **Honor**: **National Representation**
- **Theme**: *Throwing Arrow into Pot*
- **Problem**: Robots had to collect and accurately throw arrows into pots placed at different heights and distances.
- **Team POV**: Success depended on precise aiming, stable launching mechanisms, and repeatable shooting accuracy rather than just speed.
